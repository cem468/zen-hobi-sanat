Adult Studio

Sculpture & Figure Modeling Studio

Three-dimensional form work in clay and plaster, bust and figure modeling. Learn to build volume and balance with your own hands.

Sculpture

Learn to think in three dimensions

Sculpture is the art of giving material volume and balance. In our studio you'll work in clay and plaster on busts, figures and free-form pieces, sharpening both your powers of observation and your hand skills.

  • Modeling and form work in clay
  • Bust and figure studies
  • An introduction to plaster mold-making and casting
  • Observation-based study of anatomy and proportion
  • Individual attention and guidance for advanced levels

Frequently Asked Questions

What you're wondering

Observation and patience are enough. As you learn the techniques, you'll be surprised by what your hands can do.

We mainly start with clay and plaster; as you progress, you can move on to other materials as well.

Your clay pieces are dried and fired in the kiln if you'd like; plaster pieces go home with you at the end of the class.
