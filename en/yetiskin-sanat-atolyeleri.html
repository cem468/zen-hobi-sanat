Adult Art Studios

Follow one of five main paths — ceramics with clay, painting with a brush, pouring art with fluid paint, sculpture with clay and plaster, or breathe new life into an existing object with decorative painting.

Ceramics Studio

Hand-building, glazing and firing. Make your own mug, bowl and vase from scratch — our studio's most popular discipline.

Pouring Art

No brush needed — pour and tilt fluid paint to bring your own unique abstract painting to life.

Sculpture

Three-dimensional form, bust and figure modeling with clay and plaster. Learn to give volume and balance with your own hands.
