The Workshop Experience

A little escape for one evening

Don't have time for a longer course? Our workshops are just right for kids and adults alike. In a single session you'll try your hand at ceramic shaping, pouring art, candle making or decorative painting, complete your piece in that same class, and head home with your own creation.

  • Starts and finishes in a single session
  • Open to both kids and adults
  • For couples, friend groups and individuals
  • Ceramic shaping, pouring art, candle making and decorative painting options
  • All materials included

Workshop Types

Which experience will you choose?

All four are completed in a single session, with materials included. All you need to bring is yourself.

Ceramic Shaping Experience

Shape clay with your own hands to create your own mug or bowl — it's a calming, meditative experience. Your piece is dried and fired in our own studio kiln before it's delivered to you.

Pouring Art Session

Without using a brush, pour and tilt fluid paint across a canvas to reveal your own abstract painting. The result is unpredictable and deeply relaxing — you take your painting home the same day.

Explore

Candle Making Workshop

Melt natural soy wax and pour it into a mold, choosing your own color and scent. Open to both kids and adults — the whole process, from setting the wick to unmolding, is completed in a single session, and you take your own candle design home the same day.

Decorative Painting

Bring a wooden, ceramic or glass object back to life with brush and paint — even with no experience, you'll finish with a piece of your own the same day. As you paint, your mind slows down and the day's fatigue fades away — you'll head home with a handmade gift, special for you or someone you love.

Duration: 1.5–3 hours All materials included By reservation
