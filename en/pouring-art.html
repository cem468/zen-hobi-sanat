Pouring Art

No brush, just the flow of paint

Pouring art is a technique in which fluid acrylic paint is poured onto canvas in a controlled way. Because part of the outcome is left to the paint's natural flow, every painting turns out different — a unique piece each time. It requires no brush skill, making it one of the most-asked-about and most instantly satisfying techniques we teach.

  • Fluid acrylic (pouring) techniques
  • Brushless, intuitive and relaxing
  • Every result is one of a kind
  • Complete a painting in a single session, zero experience needed
